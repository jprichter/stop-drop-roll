This pitch deck is designed to help you sell the "open mat radar" concept to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) gym owners. It frames the application as a tool for **"culture control"** and **"visitor management"** rather than just a directory, addressing the specific anxieties both owners and travelers face.

---

### **Slide 1: The Problem – The "Traveler’s Friction"**
*   **The Struggle:** Travelers want to train but face "drop-in anxiety". They don't know if an open mat is actually happening, what the fee is, or if the gym has a shower.
*   **The Burden for Owners:** Gym owners deal with random DMs, "stale" information on their websites, and unexpected visitors showing up at the wrong time.
*   **The Gap:** Google Maps tells you where a gym is, but it doesn't tell you if you can train there **"comfortably and without surprises"** at 10:00 AM this Saturday.

### **Slide 2: The Solution – The "Open Mat Radar"**
*   **What it is:** A specialized temporal and logistical layer on top of Google Maps.
*   **The Vision:** An index that answers the traveler’s North Star: *"I’m in city X this weekend. Show me open mats near me between Saturday 10am–2pm"*.
*   **The Benefit:** It reduces "decision friction" by highlighting essential logistics like **drop-in fees, Gi/No-gi status, and facility icons** (🚿 🔒 🚗).

---

### **The "Gym Partner" Tiers**

#### **Tier 1: Verified (The Foundation) – Free**
*Goal: Claim your gym and ensure your data is never "stale."*
*   **Owner-Claimed Status:** Verify your gym in 60 seconds via Instagram DM or SMS (no password/account required).
*   **Real-Time Schedule Control:** Instantly update open mat times, add **holiday exceptions**, or trigger a **"Cancelled Today"** flag to protect your gym's reputation.
*   **Trust Badge:** Verified owner data sits at the top of the **Trust Hierarchy**, ensuring travelers see your info as the most accurate.
*   **Culture Descriptors:** Define your vibe (Free roll, positional rounds, typical attendance) so the right visitors find you.

#### **Tier 2: Pro (The Friction Reducer) – $ / % per Transaction**
*Goal: Eliminate "logistics anxiety" and manage visitor flow.*
*   **Integrated Payments:** Allow users to pay drop-in fees directly in the app, solving the "cash only?" or "contact gym" friction.
*   **The "Traveler’s Pass":** Visitors receive a **QR code** for instant check-in, proving they’ve paid and signed your digital waiver in advance.
*   **Visitor "Heads Up":** Receive notifications of incoming visitors, helping you manage mat space and safety.
*   **"Drop-in Ready" Certification:** A special badge for gyms that provide "clutch" amenities like **towels, showers, and easy parking**.

#### **Tier 3: Featured (The Magnet) – Monthly Subscription**
*Goal: Maximize visibility and gain market insights.*
*   **Boosted Visibility:** Your gym's pin appears in a distinct color and remains visible at higher zoom levels on the map.
*   **"Radar Insights":** Access data on how many people are searching for open mats in your city and how often your gym card is viewed.
*   **Facility Highlighting:** Prominently display your best assets (e.g., "Shower Available," "Free Parking") in search results to attract travelers.
*   **Priority Event Promotion:** Feature special seminars or holiday open mats at the top of the "Time Selector" for your region.
