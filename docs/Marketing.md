## 🛠 Data bootstrapping (how you don’t start empty)

Start with:

* Your local gyms
* Manually seed 20–50 gyms
* Add their open mats

Then:

* Share with:

  * Reddit
  * Discords
  * Competition groups
  * “Add your gym” CTA

Your hook:

> “Help build the global open mat map.”

People LOVE contributing if it helps travel.
